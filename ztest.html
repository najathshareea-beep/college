Thirunoor'25

Jamia Mahiriyya Islamic College, Ramanattukara is hosting its Islamic Art Fest "Thirunoor", symbolizing the light of Prophet Muhammad (S) and marking the 100th anniversary of Samastha Kerala Jamiyyathul Ulama.

The fest showcases student talents in arts, literature, and culture, with three teams – Isra, Hira, and Qaswa – reflecting milestones of Islamic history.
