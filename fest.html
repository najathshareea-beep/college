About അന്യോന്യം'25

"അന്യോന്യം" (Anyonyam) represents unity and mutual cooperation. This year's arts fest brings together three dynamic teams - Isra, Qaswa, and Hira - competing across various artistic and cultural domains.

The festival includes competitions in stage performances, non-stage events, group activities, and individual talents, celebrating the rich cultural heritage and creative expressions of our students.
