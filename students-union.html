Organizational Structure Details

Chain of Command
  1. President: Overall authority and final decision-maker
  2. Vice Presidents: Assist president and handle specific domains
  3. General Secretary: Administrative head and record keeper
  4. Treasurer: Financial management and budgeting
  5. Joint Secretaries: Assist general secretary
  6. Committee Conveners: Head specialized committees
  7. Committee Members: Execute committee activities
Operational Protocol
  • Monthly Executive Committee meetings
  • Bi-monthly General Body meetings
  • Committee reports submitted monthly
  • Financial audits every quarter
  • Annual election process in January
  • Transparent decision-making process
  • Documentation of all proceedings
